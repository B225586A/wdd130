Harare - My Favorite City

Harare is the capital city of Zimbabwe and my favorite city. It is known as the Sunshine City because of its beautiful weather throughout the year. The city is full of jacaranda trees that bloom with purple flowers in spring and has very friendly people.

I love Harare because it is a place where modern buildings meet beautiful natural scenery. There are many amazing places to visit like the National Heroes Acre, Harare Gardens, Mukuvisi Woodlands, and the vibrant markets. Harare is truly the heart of Zimbabwe and it inspires me.
